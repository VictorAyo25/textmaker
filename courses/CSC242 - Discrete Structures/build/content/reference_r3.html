Recall and check

1. Which of the converse, inverse and contrapositive agrees with the original conditional?

2. State the Handshaking theorem and one thing it immediately tells you.

3. When is a relation both symmetric and antisymmetric?

4. Give the two conditions for an Euler circuit and for an Euler path.

5. How many edges has a tree on 30 vertices?

6. What is the difference between the mean and the median in the presence of an outlier?

7. Name three drills that have appeared on at least three of the five papers.

Answers

1. Only the contrapositive.
2. Σ deg(v) = 2|E|. It tells you at once that the degree sum is even, so no graph can have an odd degree sum, and that the number of odd-degree vertices is even.
3. When no two different elements are related in both directions, which includes any relation made only of loops.
4. Circuit: connected and every vertex of even degree. Path: connected and exactly two vertices of odd degree.
5. 29, since e = v − 1.
6. The median is unaffected by an extreme value; the mean is dragged towards it.
7. Any three of: types of undirected graph, the minimum tour, the chain letter, Handshaking, the R1 to R6 classification, rooted-tree terminology and traversals, the digraph tabulation, balanced m-ary trees.
