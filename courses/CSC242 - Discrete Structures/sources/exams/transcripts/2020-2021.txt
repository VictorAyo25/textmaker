=== NOTES ON THIS PAPER (defects and unresolved readings) ===

1. COURSE CODE IS CSC311 on this paper, with the same title, department and
   semester. Together with CSC416 (2021/2022, 2024/2025) and CSC 242 (2023/2024,
   2025/2026) this fixes the lineage: one course, renamed twice. All five papers
   are valid practice for CSC242.

2. Q5(a)(vi) prints "Contigency". The word is "contingency", and in logic the
   term wanted is a contingency: a proposition that is neither a tautology nor a
   contradiction. Quote the misspelling, then define the real term.

3. Q3(a) and Q1(c) punctuate commands as questions ("explain ... means?").

4. THE e1 TO e6 FIGURE IN Q1(c) IS ONLY PARTLY READABLE. Vertices 1, 2, 3 and
   their edges e1 to e5 are clear, including that e1/e2 and e4/e5 are parallel
   pairs. The sixth edge, e6, is drawn as a long curve on the right of the
   figure passing around vertex 4, and at this resolution it cannot be settled
   whether it is a loop at vertex 2 or an edge from 2 to 3. Vertex 4 is isolated
   either way, which is the point the question is testing (a vertex incident to
   no edge). The manual states the reading it uses, flags the doubt, and teaches
   the method.

5. Q4(a) "Playoff between two teams consists of at most five games" is missing
   its article ("A playoff"). Part (i) says "Construct a tree diagram for five
   games", though the playoff can end in three or four; the tree must show every
   way the series can finish, not exactly five games.

6. Q2(a), Q3(b) and Q3(c) reappear almost verbatim on the 2021/2022 paper, and
   Q2(a) again on 2023/2024. The chain-letter question is the same problem as
   2021/2022 Q3(a) and 2023/2024 Q5(d), phrased three different ways: 2020/2021
   tells it as a chain letter, the later two as convocation invitation cards.
   Teach it once, then show all three phrasings.

7. Q4(b) is exactly the same question as 2021/2022 Q2(c), down to the five
   correspondence types, differing only in capitalisation.

8. Q1(a) is the same question as 2021/2022 Q1(c) and 2023/2024 Q4(a): "the types
   of undirected graph", worth 12, 12 and 9 marks respectively. This is the
   single most repeated question in the whole set.

9. Marks per question: Q1 12+6.5+5 = 23.5; Q2 10+6+7.5 = 23.5;
   Q3 7.5+8+8 = 23.5; Q4 4+3.5+10+3+3 = 23.5; Q5 6+5+5+6.5 = 22.5.
   Q5 is one mark short of the others as printed.
