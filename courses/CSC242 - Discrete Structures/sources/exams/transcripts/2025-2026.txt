=== NOTES ON THIS PAPER (defects found; they stay IN the quote) ===

1. Q1 a) ii) prints "Prepositional logic". The intended word is "Propositional".
   A preposition is a part of speech; propositional logic is the subject. Quote
   it as printed and answer the intended question.

2. Q1 a) iv) prints "Path Matrice" (singular/plural slip for "Path Matrix" or
   "Path Matrices").

3. Q1 a) v) prints "(2marks)" with no space, unlike i) to iv) which print
   "(2 marks)".

4. Q1 numbering runs "a)", "b)", then "(c)" with brackets. Q2 onward uses
   "(a)", "(b)". Inconsistent within the same question.

5. Q2 (a) reads "Provide five Justification the inclusion of Discrete structure
   to Computer Science students curriculum." A word is missing (it should read
   "five justifications FOR the inclusion") and "Justification" is capitalised
   mid-sentence.

6. Q3 (a) IS SELF-CONTRADICTORY AND IT MATTERS. It states "72 students can speak
   Ibibio and 43 students can speak Ibibio", naming the SAME language twice,
   then parts (i) to (iii) ask about Ibibio AND Efik. The second figure must be
   Efik for the question to be answerable. This is the single most important
   defect on the paper: a student who reads it literally has an unanswerable
   question and loses 7 marks. The manual quotes it as printed, flags it, states
   the reading it must be given (43 speak Efik), and answers on that reading.

7. Q3 (c) reads "10 male and 10 female students needs to pray" (subject/verb
   disagreement) and capitalises "Prayer" in (i) but not "prayer" in (ii).

8. Q4 (b) and (d) end with a question mark on sentences that are commands
   ("Discuss...?", "Find out the number of ways ... can be arranged?").

9. Q6 (b) is a run-on with a capital "In" mid-sentence: "...digits may be
   repeated In the light of the above, how many 4 to 6 digit pin codes are
   there?"

10. Q6 (c) ii "Identify the number of parts in the task and the type of the
    parts (compulsory or alternative)" and iii "Which rule applies here?" refer
    back to (b) and (c) i. The referent of "the task" is not stated.
