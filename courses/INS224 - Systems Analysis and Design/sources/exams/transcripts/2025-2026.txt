===== QUESTION TWO (Q2) =====

[[Q2]]
A patient visits a hospital to use the patient billing system in order to make enquiries about the types of drugs he can buy. He must have insurance or he won't be attended to by the Nurse. The nurse checks his vitals and refers him to a doctor who in turn refers him to the Insurance Carrier to confirm the patient has his insurance covered. On confirmation, the doctor advises the patient on which drugs to use for his condition.
Note that a patient must be assigned to only one doctor but a doctor can have many patients. A patient can have only one insurance ID to prevent insurance fraud.
a. Draw a class diagram for this scenario. [Hint: Show multiplicity where necessary] (5 mks)
b. Draw a use case diagram for the system. (5 mks)
c. Draw an activity diagram to show the system's process flow. (5 mks)
d. State three (3) drawbacks of multiple inheritance. (2.5 mks)

===== QUESTION FIVE (Q5) =====

[[Q5]]
A federal teaching hospital intends to replace its fragmented patient appointment, clinical record, laboratory request, and medication tracking systems with a single integrated Clinical Workflow and Patient Safety Management System. The current system is partly paper-based and partly supported by disconnected departmental applications. Doctors complain that patient records are incomplete, nurses report delays in medication administration, laboratory scientists complain about duplicated test requests, and hospital administrators are concerned about regulatory compliance, audit trails, and data privacy.
The hospital's board wants the system delivered within twelve months. However, stakeholders disagree on system priorities. Doctors want speed and flexibility, nurses want ease of use, laboratory staff want accurate workflow integration, and administrators insist on strict documentation, traceability, and reliability. Failure in the new system could affect patient safety.
a. Briefly compare seven (7) methodologies that can be used in this project. (7 mks)
[Hint: your answer should be short and concisely state why the methodologies may be suitable or not]
b. Identify five (5) activities in project planning. (5 mks)
c. Using the case, formulate at least:
i. two (2) business requirements,
ii. two (2) user requirements,
iii. three (3) functional requirements,
iv. two (2) non-functional requirements, and
v. two (2) system requirements, (5.5 mks)

===== QUESTION SIX (Q6) =====

[[Q6]]
A private university wants to build an AI-supported programming learning platform for undergraduate students. The system should help students learn Python, Java, and C++ by providing coding exercises, automated feedback, debugging support, lecturer dashboards, plagiarism alerts, and adaptive learning recommendations. The university expects the system to support 8,000 students across three campuses.
At the start of the project, requirements are unclear. Students want instant answers to coding problems, lecturers want the system to promote independent thinking rather than answer copying, administrators want analytics on student performance, and the ICT unit is concerned about security, scalability, integration with the learning management system, and maintenance cost. Halfway through the project, university management requests additional features: AI-generated lecture notes, automatic grading of essays, and integration with the university payment portal.

b. Design a requirements elicitation plan for the system using at least four techniques. For each technique, identify the stakeholders involved, the type of information to be collected, and why the technique is suitable for this project. (4 mks).
c. Write short notes on: Unified Modeling Language (UML) (also state the inventors and its uses). (2 mks)
d. Categories UML diagrams into two broad categories. Mention two diagrams in each of the categories and describe it function (4 mks)
e. With the aid of diagrams, explain the following class relationships in system analysis: (i) Inheritance, (ii) Composition, (iii) Aggregation, and (iv) Association. (4 mks)
