[[Q1]]
As a systems analyst working on a new library management system, you are tasked with gathering system requirements from various stakeholders.

i. Three functional requirements. (3 Marks)
ii. Three non-functional requirements. (3 Marks)

ii. You are nearing the end of the project and the client is insisting on adding new features but you want to prevent scope and feature creep. Do you refuse or agree to add the new features? Give three cogent reasons for your decision as Project Manager. (3 Marks)

d. A warehouse has recently enforced a new management system to streamline the receiving process of stock. When truck drivers arrive, they are required to submit their trucks to the warehouse. On submission, the warehouse manager instructs the manual laborer to unload the trucks. It is important to note that a truck may or may not be carrying any stock.
Once unloading is complete, the storekeeper is responsible for taking inventory of all available stock (spoilt, missing and in-good condition). The storekeeper then generates a comprehensive stock report, which is disseminated to the warehouse manager, the laborer involved in the unloading and the truck driver.
Separately, both the truck drivers (regarding their delivery completion) and the labourers (regarding their unloading tasks) are required to submit individual activity reports directly to the manager.
i. Draw an ERD to model the relationships between all identified entities. (5 marks)
ii. Draw a Level 0 DFD to model the various processes involved. (4.5 marks)
e. After drawing the Data flow diagrams for the above scenario, outline three (3) ways you can validate the integrity and correctness of the diagrams (1.5 marks)
