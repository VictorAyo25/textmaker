M.1 · MOCK A · BEFORE YOU START

Mock Paper A, in the older shape

Six questions, any four, three hours. This is the 2024/25 shape, and it is the shape that rewards choosing well.

MUST-MEMORISE · How to sit this paper

Time allowed: 3 hours. Attempt any four (4) questions. Each question carries 17.5 marks. Total: 70 marks.

Every question follows the same shape: A define (3), B debug (3), C dry run (4), D write a program (7.5).

MEMORY HOOK: four of six, and D is 7.5 of every 17.5. Nearly half of this paper is writing programs. Budget 45 minutes a question and spend half of it on D.

TEACH · Sit it properly, or do not sit it

Book shut. Paper and pen. A clock you can see. Three hours, in one sitting, with no compiler. If you look up one method signature mid-paper, the result tells you nothing you can use, because the hall has no lookups.

Answer four questions, as instructed, and note which two you dropped. Then, afterwards and untimed, answer the other two anyway. The ones you avoid are a map of what you have not learned yet, and next year's paper is written by someone who does not know which two you like.

TRAP · Choosing four is itself a skill, and it is timed

Read all six before writing anything. Five minutes spent choosing is the best-paid five minutes on the paper, because part D is 7.5 marks and it is where a bad choice costs you twenty minutes you cannot get back.

Choose on part D alone. Parts A, B and C are 10 marks you can attempt on any topic; D is where a question is won or abandoned. If you cannot see the shape of D's program in your head within a minute, that is not your question.
