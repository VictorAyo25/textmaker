The Must-Memorise Consolidation

The enumerated lists, in one place. These are where the fill-in answers and the "which of the following" options come from. Cover the right-hand side and recite.

Must memoriseDefinitions to quote
  • Agriculture: the science, art, and practice of cultivating plants and raising animals for food, fibre, medicine.
  • Agripreneurship: entrepreneurship in agriculture; a business mindset brought to agriculture.
  • Value chain: the entire series of activities and transactions needed to make a product and deliver it to consumers.
  • Aquaculture: controlled rearing of aquatic plants and animals for commercial, recreational, and scientific purposes. Fish farming (Pisciculture): the science and art of rearing fish.
  • Value addition: transforming a product from its original state to a more valuable one, by altering place, time, or form.
Must memoriseFish farming (Module Two)
  • Five departments: Hatchery (labour room), Grow-out, Processing, Feed mill (kitchen), Administrative.
  • Three water parameters: Physical (temperature, turbidity), Chemical (pH 6.5 to 8.5; DO 5 to 8 mg/L; ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, iron), Biological (plankton, bacteria such as Aeromonas and Pseudomonas; TBC high risk above 1.0x105 CFU/mL).
  • Four behaviour patterns: Inability to eat, Hungry (feed to satiation), Slow movement (increase aeration), Stress (clamped fins, faded colour).
  • 3M: Make (science: 10 principles), Manage (art: HR, records), Multiply (art: 7Ps, 4Cs). 7Ps: product, promotion, place, price, physical evidence, people, process. 4Cs: customer, cost, convenience, communication. Research pair: SWOT and SMART.
  • Indices for exploitation: high population, high demand, insufficient production, abundant water and land, low supply.
Must memoriseOil palm (Module Three)
  • Elaeis guineensis. Temp 25 to 34 C, rainfall 2000 to 2500 mm, soil pH 5.0 to 6.0.
  • Dura x Pisifera gives Tenera (commercial). Dormancy: 40 C for 70 to 80 days. Nursery 12 to 15 months. Spacing 9 m x 9 m triangular, ~143 palms/hectare. Fertiliser NPKMg (12:12:17:2). First fruit 3 to 4 years.
  • Fruit types (external): Nigrescens, Virescens, Albescens. Species (internal): Dura, Pisifera, Tenera.
  • Five processing stages: Sterilisation (140 to 150 C, 60 to 90 min), Threshing, Digesting, Pressing (crude palm oil), Clarification.
  • By-products: EFB (fertiliser), PKC (animal feed), POME (wastewater, high BOD/COD).
Must memoriseAgribusiness (Module Four)
  • Six evaluation criteria: market demand, cost of inputs, profit margin, scalability, environmental and social impact, policy and regulatory compliance.
  • Six value-addition types: form, place, time, convenience, packaging and branding, nutritional. Three forms: primary, secondary, tertiary.
  • Four innovation types: product, process, marketing, organisational.
  • POCD (operations scope): Planning, Organising, Controlling, Directing.

Glossary

Every term the paper might turn into a fill-in question, defined in one line.

TeachA to L
TermMeaning
AgripreneurSomeone who spots agricultural opportunities and builds profitable, sustainable ventures.
Aquaponic farmingFish production combined with growing plants in a linked water-based system.
BiosecurityMeasures to prevent the spread of disease and pests on a farm.
ClarificationHeating and skimming crude palm oil to separate it from water and solids.
DigestingMilling sterilised fruit to separate the mesocarp from the kernel.
Dura / Pisifera / TeneraOil palm species by shell: thick / thin or none / the recommended hybrid.
EutrophicationOver-enrichment of water with nutrients, causing algal overgrowth.
Fingerlings / JuvenilesYoung fish; juveniles are larger than fingerlings.
HatcheryThe fish-farm unit ("labour room") where fingerlings and juveniles are produced.
HypoxiaA deficiency of oxygen (in water, low dissolved oxygen).
IntercroppingGrowing other crops (cassava, maize) among young oil palms.
TeachM to Z
TermMeaning
MesocarpThe fleshy middle layer of the oil palm fruit; source of palm oil.
PiscicultureThe branch of aquaculture dealing with rearing fish.
POMEPalm oil mill effluent: wastewater high in organic matter; must be treated.
PropagationReproducing oil palm, usually by seed (or tissue culture).
SatiationFeeding fish to fullness, to avoid cannibalism.
SterilisationSteaming or boiling fresh fruit bunches to kill pathogens and loosen fruits.
Stocking densityThe number of fish kept per unit area or volume of water.
ThreshingRemoving fruits from the sterilised bunch stalks.
Triangular spacingPlanting each palm at equal distance forming an equilateral triangle.
TurbidityThe cloudiness of water from suspended particles (a physical parameter).
Value chainThe full series of activities needed to make a product and deliver it to consumers.
