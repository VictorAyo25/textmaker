As printedTest 1 Q1
In terms of physico-chemical parameters, which of the following represents the "Ideal Range" for Dissolved Oxygen (DO) to ensure healthy fish growth? a. 25°C – 30°C b. < 0.05 mg/L c. 6.5 – 8.5 mg/L d. 5 – 8 mg/L
Break it down

DO minimum is 5 to 8 mg/L. Option a is a temperature range; c is the pH range (6.5 to 8.5) baited with a mg/L unit; b is the ammonia toxicity threshold.

Answerd

d, 5 to 8 mg/L. Do not confuse DO (5 to 8) with pH (6.5 to 8.5).
