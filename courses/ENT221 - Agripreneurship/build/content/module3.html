Module Three · Unit 1

Oil Palm Cultivation

From seed to harvest: the crop, the conditions it needs, the varieties, and the agronomy. Every underlined number here is the kind a CBT turns into a question.

Must memoriseThe crop and its conditions

Elaeis guineensis is the oil palm. It gives palm oil (from the mesocarp, the fleshy part) and palm kernel oil (from the kernel). Optimal growing conditions:

  • Temperature: 25 to 34 C
  • Annual rainfall: 2000 to 2500 mm
  • Soil pH: 5.0 to 6.0 (slightly acidic)
TeachText 3.1.1

Cultivation runs through five stages: propagation, nursery management, field establishment, agronomic practices, and harvesting. The figure lays them on a timeline; the boxes below give the numbers for each.

Must memoriseThe numbers
  • Propagation: by seed. Break dormancy by treating seeds at 40 C for 70 to 80 days. Dura x Pisifera gives the Tenera hybrid (highest oil yield).
  • Nursery: seedlings in black polythene bags for 12 to 15 months; fertiliser at 56 g per bag.
  • Field: spacing 9 m x 9 m triangular, giving about 143 palms per hectare. Plant at the start of the rains.
  • Agronomy: weeding, fertiliser NPKMg (12:12:17:2), pruning, pest and disease control.
  • Harvest: first fruit 3 to 4 years after planting. Ready signs: loose fruits falling, colour change, softening. Tools: chisel, harvesting hook, climbing rope, mechanical harvester.
TeachFruit types vs species

Two different classifications, and telling them apart is a classic exam trap. Fruit types describe the outside (colour and morphology). Species describe the inside (the shell, genetic).

TrapType is not species

Nigrescens, Virescens and Albescens are fruit types (colour). Dura, Pisifera and Tenera are species (shell). A question that describes "black turning red when ripe" wants Nigrescens, not Dura; one that says "thick shell, no fibre ring" wants Dura, not Nigrescens. Keep the two columns apart in your head.

Must memoriseProducts and by-products
  • Palm oil (from the mesocarp): food, cosmetics, biofuel.
  • Palm kernel oil (from the kernel): frying, soap, cosmetics.
  • Palm kernel cake (PKC): livestock feed, organic fertiliser.
  • Palm pressed fibre (PPF): biomass energy, animal bedding.
  • Empty fruit bunches (EFB): organic fertiliser, bioenergy, biodegradable materials.

Module Three · Unit 2

Oil Palm Processing

Turning fresh fruit bunches into oil is five ordered steps. Learn the order and what each does; the order itself is a favourite question.

Must memoriseThe five processing stages, in order
  1. Sterilisation: boil the fresh fruit bunches (FFB) with steam, about 140 to 150 C for 60 to 90 minutes. It kills pathogens, inactivates enzymes, loosens fruits, and limits free fatty acids.
  2. Threshing: remove the fruits from the sterilised bunch stalks.
  3. Digesting: mill the fruits to break the cell walls and separate the mesocarp from the kernel (de-pulping).
  4. Pressing / extraction: press the digested pulp (screw, hydraulic, or centrifugal press) to release crude palm oil (CPO).
  5. Clarification: mix the crude oil with water and heat, then skim to separate solids and sludge from the oil.
TeachKernel, quality, by-products

Palm kernel is processed separately: separate the fibre, dry the nuts (sun or mechanical), crack them, extract the oil, and store (nuts in jute bags, oil in tanks).

Oil quality depends on harvesting time, sterilisation time and temperature, pressing conditions, and storage and handling.

By-products: EFB (fertiliser, bioenergy), PKC (animal feed), and POME, palm oil mill effluent, a wastewater with high BOD and COD that must be treated before discharge (it can be turned into biogas or compost).

PickPractice

Which sequence correctly orders the processing of palm fruit into crude oil?

  • Threshing, sterilisation, pressing, digesting, clarification
  • Sterilisation, threshing, digesting, pressing, clarification
  • Digesting, pressing, sterilisation, threshing, clarification
  • Sterilisation, digesting, threshing, clarification, pressing

Answer b. Sterilise first (you cannot thresh a raw bunch well), then thresh, digest, press for crude palm oil, and clarify. The order is the question.
