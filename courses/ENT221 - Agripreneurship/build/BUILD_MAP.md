### Module One - Agriculture & Agripreneurship (text pp2-7; §1.1.1-1.1.3)
Core ideas / MUST-MEMORISE:
- Agriculture = the science, art, and practice of cultivating plants and raising animals for food, fibre, medicine.
- 11 aspects: Crop Production, Animal Husbandry, Soil Management, Irrigation/Water Mgmt, Agricultural Engineering, Agroforestry, Agricultural Economics, Food Science & Tech, Sustainable/Organic Farming, **Plant Breeding** (new crop varieties -> T1Q18), Pest & Disease Management.
- Agripreneurship = entrepreneurship in agriculture; "bringing a business mindset to agriculture" (T2Q15 = business). Agripreneur = identifies opportunities + entrepreneurial skills.
- Key components: Innovation, Value Addition, Sustainability, Agribusiness Management, Diversification, Risk Management, Tech Integration.
- Value chain = "the entire series of activities and transactions needed to make a product and deliver it to consumers" (T1Q3). Segments incl. Production; **Processing** = transform raw matured products into value-added consumable/industrial goods via milling/smoking/canning (T1Q12); Marketing; Distribution; Ag Finance; Ag Technology; Ag Education; Ag Policy/Advocacy; **Ag Waste Management** = recycle waste into biofuels/animal feeds (T1Q6); Ag Infrastructure; Ag Tourism; Ag Consulting.
- Importance: Economic Growth, **Food Security** (T2Q8 True), Sustainability.
- Interconnected sectors: livestock+crop linked (crop residues feed livestock; manure -> soil; soils -> crops) (T2Q18).

### Module Two - Fish Farming (text pp9-32; §2.1.1-2.1.9) [HEAVIEST tested]
- Aquaculture = controlled cultivation/rearing of aquatic plants & animals in water for commercial, recreational, scientific purposes.
- Fish farming = branch of aquaculture (**Pisciculture**) = the **science and art** of rearing fish (T1Q13; T2Q16 = art).
- 5 departments: **Hatchery** (labour room) = fingerlings/juveniles (T2Q21); Grow-out; Processing; **Feed mill (kitchen)** = fish feed (T2Q23); Administrative.
- Investment potential; feed = 60-70% of production costs; **Fish feed production** is an opportunity (T1Q28).
- Indices for exploitation: 1) High population 2) High demand for fish 3) Insufficient production of farmed fishes 4) Abundant water & land resources 5) Low supply of fishes (T1Q16 = insufficient production + high population; T1Q29 = high demand; T2Q3).
- WATER QUALITY (3 parameters):
  - PHYSICAL: Temperature (tilapia 25-30C), Turbidity/suspended solids, odour, colour (T1Q2; T2Q26).
  - CHEMICAL: **pH 6.5-8.5** (T2Q22=8.5); **DO 5-8 mg/L** (T1Q1=d); Ammonia >0.05 toxic; Nitrite <0.1; Nitrate >50; CO2 >20; Iron safe <0.1 / moderate 0.1-0.5 / high >0.5. Chemical = ammonia, salinity, pH, hardness, dissolved minerals (T2Q5).
  - BIOLOGICAL: Plankton; Microbial (harmful **Aeromonas, Vibrio, Pseudomonas**; beneficial Nitrosomonas/Nitrobacter); Pathogens/Parasites; Organic matter; Macrophytes. TBC High Risk **> 1.0x10^5 CFU/mL** (T1Q20). Excess bacteria incl. Aeromonas & Pseudomonas (T2Q24).
  - Thumb rule: do not venture into fish farming without knowing the quality of your water (T1Q21; T2Q25).
- Aquarium mgmt: Setup (substrate/decorations, condition water, glass sealing, **know fish species** -> T2Q9,Q10); Daily (feed 1-2x, avoid overfeeding -> **water pollution** T2Q20); Weekly (**partial water changes 20-30%**, clean glass/decorations -> T1Q30 weekly; T2Q7 "water"); Monthly (filter, inspect); Long-term (quarantine, trim).
- Fish behaviour: INABILITY TO EAT (reject feed+gasp+slow = polluted water/illness T1Q25); HUNGRY (begging/lunging -> feed to **satiation** to avoid cannibalism T1Q5); SLOW MOVEMENT (increase **aeration** air stones/filters/air pumps T1Q17); STRESS (clamped fins, faded colour, secluded corner = Stress T1Q7; sudden temp fluctuation "shock"/improper acclimatisation T1Q22).
- 3M of wealth = **MAKE, MANAGE, MULTIPLY** (Make=science; Manage/Multiply=art) (T1Q19=Make).
  - MAKE (10 principles): Water, Land, Security, Location, Fisheries knowledge & manpower, Source of fingerlings/juveniles, Feed availability & quality, Market, Construction, Finance (T2Q28).
  - MANAGE: records, financial prudence, communication, diligence, **Effective HR Mgmt** = code of conduct, policy, regulation, discipline (T1Q26).
  - MULTIPLY: **Technological input** + networking + 7Ps (T1Q9 = Multiply phase); Leverage/Networking; **7Ps** (product, promotion, place, price, physical evidence/packaging, people, process/positioning); **4Cs** (customer, cost, convenience, communication) (T1Q11=7Ps&4Cs); Investment; Reward mechanism; Integrated approach.
  - R&D: SWOT + SMART analysis (T2Q4). Be creative, maximise resources, reduce cost, increase output (T2Q11).
- Marketing fresh/processed fish = good product quality + strong branding + smart sales (T1Q10). Target markets: **Local Consumers** = households, restaurants, hotels, supermarkets (T1Q24); Wholesale; Online; Export. First step = identify target **market** (T2Q27). Brand: USP + Attractive Packaging + **Hygiene** & Quality Assurance (T1Q14). Value addition: smoked, spiced, fillets, frozen, marinated/barbeque (T1Q27).

### Module Three - Oil Palm (text pp35-48) [teach+test FULLY per directive]
Unit 1 Cultivation (§3.1.1-3.1.6):
- **Elaeis guineensis**. Optimal: Temp 25-34C, Rainfall 2000-2500mm, Soil pH 5.0-6.0.
- Stages: Propagation, Nursery, Field establishment, Agronomic practices, Harvesting.
- **Dura (DD) x Pisifera (PF) -> Tenera (DP)** = most commercial. Break dormancy: **40C for 70-80 days**.
- Nursery: black polythene bags, **12-15 months**; fertilizer 56g/bag.
- Field: spacing **9m x 9m triangular = ~143 palms/hectare**.
- Agronomic: weeding, fertilizer **NPKMg (12:12:17:2)**, pruning, pest/disease control. Pests: bagworms, rhinoceros beetles, red spider mites, rodents, freckle disease, Ganoderma trunk rot.
- Harvest: fruit **3-4 years** after planting; signs (loose fruits, colour change, softening); tools (chisel, harvesting hook, climbing rope, mechanical harvester).
- Products: palm oil (mesocarp), palm kernel oil (kernel), palm kernel cake (feed/fertilizer), palm pressed fibre (biomass/bedding), EFB.
- Fruit types (external morphology): Nigrescens (black->red), Virescens (green->reddish orange), Albescens (white->pale yellow), Mantle/Poisson, Parthenocarpic (seedless).
- Species (internal/genetic): Dura (thick shell, no fibre ring), Pisifera (thin/no shell, poor fertilisation), Tenera (recommended).
- Intercrop first 4 yrs: cassava, maize, sweet potatoes, melon.
Unit 2 Processing (§3.2.1-3.2.5):
- Palm fruit -> oil: a) **Sterilisation** (boil; 140-150C, 60-90 min; disinfect/loosen/reduce microbes/control FFA); b) **Threshing**; c) **Digesting** (de-pulping, separate mesocarp from kernel); d) **Pressing** (screw/hydraulic/centrifugal -> crude palm oil CPO); e) **Clarification** (water+heat, skim).
- Palm kernel: fibre separation, nut drying (sun/mechanical), cracking, oil extraction, storage (jute bags / oil tanks).
- Factors affecting oil quality: harvesting time, sterilisation time/temp, pressing conditions, storage/handling.
- By-products: **EFB** (fertiliser/bioenergy), **PKC** (animal feed), **POME** (wastewater; high BOD/COD; treat before discharge; biogas/compost).

### Module Four - Agribusiness Opportunities & Value Addition (text pp52-70)
Unit 1 Opportunities (§4.1.1-4.1.6):
- Evaluation criteria: Market Demand, Cost of Inputs, Profit Margin, Scalability, Environmental/Social Impact, Policy/Regulatory Compliance.
- Value chain components: Input Supply, Production, Harvesting & Primary Processing, Storage & Packaging, Transportation & Logistics, Secondary Processing/Value Addition, Marketing & Sales, Retail & Consumption, Support Services.
- Common problems: Limited Market Access, Post-Harvest Losses, Low Productivity, Input Supply Challenges, Poor Extension Services (+ climate, financing, land tenure).
- Market needs: traceability, sustainable packaging, convenience foods, organic, plant-based proteins.
- Trends: Precision agriculture, Urban/Vertical farming, Water Conservation/Irrigation Tech, Data-Driven decisions.
Unit 2 Value Addition (§4.2.1-4.2.5):
- Value addition = transform product from original to more valuable state (shelf life/quality/new uses) by altering place, time, or form.
- Types: FORM, PLACE, TIME, CONVENIENCE, PACKAGING & BRANDING, NUTRITIONAL.
- Forms: PRIMARY (clean/sort/grade), SECONDARY (dry/mill/juice), TERTIARY (package/label/retail).
- Innovation types: Product, Process, Marketing, Organisational. Drivers: tech, consumer prefs, competition, policy.
Unit 3 Production & Operations Mgmt (§4.3.1-4.3.4):
- Production = growing crops/rearing animals; Operations = planning, input mgmt, processing, logistics, quality control.
- Components: Production Planning, Operations Scheduling/Control, Supply chain/Logistics, Tech/Innovation, Cost Control/Efficiency, Compliance/Standards (+ SAQ set: Product Design, Process Design, Capacity Planning, Inventory Mgmt).
- Scope **POCD**: Planning, Organising, Controlling, Directing.
- Advantages: productivity/profit, quality/consistency, resource use, scalability. Disadvantages: weather, labour shortage, price fluctuation, infrastructure, post-harvest losses.
- Tech: precision agriculture (drones/sensors/GPS), IoT/smart farming, farm mgmt software, automated machinery.
