SLIDE 6
Propagation
Oil palm propagated mainly by seeds
Dormancy broken at 40°C for 70–80 days
Hybrid Tenera (Dura × Pisifera) preferred
Nursery Management
Seedlings raised 12–15 months
Use perforated black polythene bags
Apply fertilizer (56 g/seedling)
Mulching, shading, watering, pest control
ccodel.covenantuniversity.edu.ng

Field Establishment
Clear vegetation and prepare land
Spacing: 9 m × 9 m triangular
Approx. 143 palms per hectare
Plant at beginning of rainy season
