| Stage | Focus | Source |
|---|---|---|

- Each stage depends on the one before it. Together, they form a complete architecture for Peak Thinking and Creative Problem-Solving.

## S4 — STAGE W: Winning Wisdom (Oyedepo) — Wisdom as Foundation
- The Central Claim: "Winning wisdom is the Supernatural Ability to bring solutions to matters too difficult for the ordinary human mind." (Dr. David Oyedepo)
- What this means for problem-solving:
  - The greatest answers are **received** by the prepared thinker, not manufactured (Jam 1:5).
  - Wisdom is not the reward of more effort; it is the fruit of **divine connection**.
  - Before you develop your strategy, go to your Source, God.

## S5 — WISDOM: IS AND IS NOT — "Wisdom Is Not Mental Exertion" (according to Dr Oyedepo)
- Wisdom is NOT: Thinking Harder; Researching More; Being more Disciplined.
- Wisdom IS: "Uncovering God's approach to every issue of life"; knowing which way to go **before** the path is fully visible; the **application** of knowledge, not its mere accumulation.
- Quote: "The application of knowledge is what is called wisdom. Until a man amasses adequate knowledge, there will be nothing for him to use." (Dr Oyedepo)

## S6 — THE KNOWLEDGE-WISDOM LADDER (3 rungs, ASCEND)
1. **INFORMATION** — gathering facts, data and experience. *Where most begin.*
2. **KNOWLEDGE** — organising and understanding information. *Many stop here.*
3. **WISDOM** — applying knowledge to produce results. *Most people never reach this level.*
- Key Insight: Knowledge gives you **power**. Wisdom gives you **strength**. The leap from knowledge to wisdom requires more than study; it requires **seeking**.

## S7 — STAGE I: Inner Architecture (Leaf) — I — Inner Architecture
- The Core Discovery: Every thought you think **physically changes the structure of your brain** (Rom 12:2).
- The brain is not fixed; it is a living, dynamic architecture you are constantly **building or dismantling**.
- You are not a product of your **Neurology** — you are its **Architect**.
- The mind that is prepared to think well is the mind positioned to **receive wisdom**.
- The Critical Link: Dr. Oyedepo says wisdom must be *Received*. Leaf shows us the **Mechanism**.

## S8 — THE SWITCH PROTOCOL — "The 21-Day Brain Detox" (Dr. Leaf's SWITCH Process)
| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| S — Gather | Become aware of the toxic thought |
| W — Focused Reflection | Examine its root and emotional charge |
| I — Write | Externalise it in a journal |
| T — Revisit | Review patterns and connections |
| C — Active Reach | Choose and rehearse the replacement thought |
| H — Celebrate | Acknowledge growth, reinforce the new pathway |
- This is not motivational self-talk. It is a **scientifically grounded rewiring protocol**. Each cycle literally builds **new neural structures**.

## S9 — STAGE S: Spirit of Possibility (Peale) — The Power of Positive Thinking
- The Central Claim: "What you consistently believe about your situation determines what you will experience." (Norman Vincent Peale)
- Peale's role in the W.I.S.E. Model: Oyedepo establishes the *source*; Leaf prepares the *receiver*; Peale **fuels the engine**.
- A mind architecturally rebuilt (Leaf) but filled with doubt will not generate the confident, exploratory energy that **creative problem-solving demands**.

## S10 — THE FIVE PRINCIPLES OF POSITIVE THINKING (Five Principles That Power Creative Breakthroughs)
| Principle | What it means |
|---|---|
| Empty the mind of fear | Fear and faith cannot coexist; fear is a cognitive contraction, not just an emotion |
| Visualise the outcome | Vivid mental rehearsal primes the brain for the result before it arrives |
| Affirm what you intend to become | The subconscious accepts what is repeatedly impressed upon it |
| Overcome the inferiority complex | The person who lacks *inner permission* will resist a breakthrough even when it arrives |
| Practise energised enthusiasm | Sustained creative work demands sustained positive energy |

## S11 — FEAR, FAITH, AND CREATIVITY — "Fear, Faith, and the Creative Mind" (The Peale-Leaf-Oyedepo Connection)
- Why Attitude Is Infrastructure:
  - Peale said: Fear and faith cannot occupy the mind simultaneously, choose faith.
  - Leaf confirmed: Fear neurologically suppresses the prefrontal cortex, the very organ of **creative thinking**.
  - Dr. Oyedepo added: "Fear will come the way of people who don't regard God's Word, nor take His counsel seriously."
- The unified message: Fear is not a feeling to be managed; it is a **cognitive stance to be dismantled**. All three authors prescribe **deliberate, active replacement**, not passive waiting for confidence to arrive.

## S12 — STAGE E: Lateral Thinking (de Bono) — E — Execution: Think Differently
- The Problem de Bono Identified: Most problems persist not because of lack of intelligence, but because of **over-commitment to an existing pattern of thinking**.
| Vertical Thinking | Lateral Thinking |
|---|---|
| Builds on what is known | Challenges what is known |
| Seeks the one correct answer | Explores many possible answers |
| Efficient *within* a pattern | **Escapes** patterns entirely |
- The solutions too difficult for the ordinary mind cannot be reached by digging deeper in the same rut.

## S13 — FOUR LATERAL THINKING TECHNIQUES (Four Tools for Escaping the Rut, Edward de Bono)
1. **Random Entry** — introduce a random word and force a connection to the problem; the arbitrary link opens pathways the brain has never travelled.
2. **The Provocative Operation (Po)** — make a deliberate, absurd statement about the problem to disrupt pattern-lock. "Po: our product has no users." Follow the implications.
3. **Challenge Assumptions** — surface every unexamined assumption beneath the problem and ask: "Why must this be this way?"
4. **The Six Thinking Hats** — White (facts) · Red (emotion) · Black (caution) · Yellow (optimism) · Green (creativity) · Blue (process).

## S14 — STAGE E: Technique for Producing Ideas (Young) — E — Execution: Produce Ideas Systematically
- The Central Claim: The production of ideas is not a gift. It is a **process**. Anyone willing to follow it faithfully will produce ideas. (James Webb Young)
- Two Foundational Principles:
  1. **New Combination** — an idea is nothing more than a new combination of existing elements.
  2. **See Relationships** — the ability to form new combinations depends on the capacity to see relationships between things that appear unrelated.
- This is de Bono's lateral thinking expressed as a creative workflow, and Dr. Oyedepo's Knowledge-Wisdom Ladder expressed as a repeatable system.

## S15 — YOUNG'S 5-STEP CREATIVE PROCESS (The Five Steps to Every Original Idea)
| Step | Action | W.I.S.E. Connection |
|---|---|---|
| Gather Raw Material | Collect specific and general knowledge voraciously | Dr. Oyedepo's knowledge base |
| Digest the Material | Turn it over in your mind; let connections form | Leaf's focused reflection |
| Drop the Problem | Release it entirely; let the subconscious work | Peale's trust and release |
| The Eureka Moment | Capture the idea the instant it arrives | de Bono's new vantage point |
| Shape and Develop | Refine through critical analysis and collaboration | Vertical thinking takes over |

## S16 — THE COMPLETE CREATIVE CYCLE (de Bono + Young)
- How they work together:
  - Young provides the *5-step process*, the architecture of idea production.
  - de Bono supplies tools, especially in Steps 1 and 2, to break cognitive locks and broaden thinking.
  - In Step 5, Young relies on de Bono's Vertical Thinking to refine the raw idea.
- de Bono teaches you how to think **differently**. Young teaches you how to produce ideas **systematically**.

## S17 — WHAT ALL FIVE AUTHORS AGREE ON (Across 5 decades, 5 disciplines, the same 5 convictions)
1. **The Mind is Primary** — external circumstances matter far less than the quality of inner life.
2. **Patterns of Thought Can Be Changed** — intelligence and potential are developed, not fixed.
3. **Insight Is Prepared For, Not Waited For** — breakthroughs reward readiness, not passive waiting.
4. **Discomfort Is Part of the Process** — growth always passes through resistance the undisciplined mind avoids.
5. **Wisdom, Thinking, and Creativity Are Not Separate Domains** — they are one integrated capacity.

## S18 — THE W.I.S.E. MODEL: THE FULL PICTURE (Putting It All Together) [flow diagram W→I→S→E]
- **W — WISDOM:** Seek the source above strategy. Dr. David O. Oyedepo, Winning Wisdom. Knowledge + Application = Wisdom. *Solutions too difficult for the ordinary human mind.*
- **I — INNER ARCHITECTURE:** Prepare the receiver. Dr. Caroline Leaf, Switch On Your Brain. Neuroplasticity · 21-Day SWITCH Protocol. *Detox · Rewire · Rebuild.*
- **S — SPIRIT OF POSSIBILITY:** Fuel the process. Norman Vincent Peale, The Power of Positive Thinking. Believe · Affirm · Visualise · Act. *Defeat Fear · Energised Enthusiasm.*
- **E — EXECUTION: THINK & CREATE:** Channel wisdom into solutions. Lateral Thinking · Edward de Bono (Random Entry · Po · Challenge Assumptions · Six Hats). Five-Step Process · James Webb Young (Gather · Digest · Incubate · Eureka · Develop). *Ideas are new combinations, a learnable process.*
- The W.I.S.E. Model is not a sequence to complete once; it is a cycle to inhabit continuously.
