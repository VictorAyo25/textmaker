# Lecture Four — Systems for Sustainable Success (20 slides)

Theme: The 7-Kit Task System (all seven kits start with "T"). Presenter on cover: Dr Azubuike Ezenwoke, PhD.

## S1 — Title
TMC221 · Lecture 4 · Systems for Sustainable Success.

## S2 — "Why Success Needs a System" (Luck vs. Cause and Effect)
- "Shallow men think of luck, but wise and strong men believe in cause and effect." (Ralph Waldo Emerson)
- Success is not **Accidental**. It runs on a **system**:
  - A system is parts working together to deliver a guaranteed result.
  - Hard work without a system is an engine with no transmission.
  - When the seven kits are assembled, success becomes a harvest.

## S3 — "The 7 Kits at a Glance" (The Task System is made up of seven interlocking kits)
1. **Task** — Am I bound to this?
2. **Training** — Am I equipped?
3. **Target** — Where exactly am I going?
4. **Tools** — What do I need in my hand?
5. **Timing** — When do I step out?
6. **Tillage** — Am I working?
7. **Tarrying** — Will I wait for it?
- Remove one kit, the engine sputters. Install all 7, success becomes inevitable.

## S4 — "Kit 1, Task" (Necessity Is Laid Upon Me)
- "Woe is unto me, if I preach not the gospel!" (1 Cor 9:16)
- Until you see your **assignment** as a necessity:
  - It will never enjoy your best.
  - You will never get the best out of it.
  - You function as one merely attempting, not accomplishing.
- Necessity is the mother of **Invention**, and Invention is the mother of **Distinction**.

## S5 — "Attempt vs. Task" (Two Mindsets, Two Outcomes)
| Attempt | Task |
|---|---|
| "Let me try this" | "This must work" |
| Retreat is an option | No retreat available |
| Dies at first resistance | Outlasts opposition |
| Ends in retirement | Ends in triumph |
- If you are going out to try, you may die in trials. Rather, make your quest a necessity.

## S6 — "The Bond of Necessity" (Lessons from History and Scripture)
- Nigeria Civil War: "To keep Nigeria one is a task that must be done." Every resource was channeled to the task.
- Apostle Paul: "For to me to live is Christ, and to die is gain." (Phil. 1:21)
- Dr. Oyedepo's own covenant: "Lord, should I pretend not to have heard You, see to it that I don't succeed in any other thing I do."
- Bind yourself by necessity, or die in bondage.

## S7 — "Kit 2, Training" (Quality of Training = Degree of Triumph)
- "Abram armed his trained servants... and pursued them unto Dan." (Gen 14:14)
- Two forms of training:
  - **Formal** — received from educational institutions; sets the pace.
  - **Informal (Personal Development)** — delivers the goods.
- Many are schooled. Few are trained. Training is proof of **Personal Commitment**.

## S8 — "Giants Built by Personal Development" (Qualification Is No Substitute, Nor Disqualifier)
- God requires that one first be trained before he is placed. Your **Training** determines your **Placement**.
- **Benjamin Franklin** — stopped school at age 10. Became inventor, diplomat, framer of the U.S. Constitution, founder of the University of Pennsylvania.
- **Richard Branson** — school drop-out turned Virgin empire founder. Not a brain drop-out.
- **Anthony Robbins** — high school education only. Now teaches professors.
- **Oral Roberts** — high school only. Founded a leading Christian University.

## S9 — "Kit 3, Target" (Certainty of Goal)
- "If you don't have a goal, you will live like a goat, looking for the path of least resistance." (Dr. David Oyedepo)
- A target must be:
  - **Specific** — not vague.
  - **Defined** — clearly described.
  - **Quantifiable** — measurable progress.
  - **Yours** — born of your own calling.
- To live without a target is to end up in tears.

## S10 — "Running a Vision With Precision" (No Goalpost, No Game)
- How anointed or talented you are notwithstanding, without a goal, you cannot score one.
- A football match without **goalposts** never ends.
- Without a **target**, you copy what is **popular**.
- Your friend starts a crusade, you join; another opens a shop, you follow; a third returns to school, you enrol. You end up disjointed.
- Always ask: "What am I actually after?"
- Stop fighting as one beating the air. Run with the tape as your target.

## S11 — "Kit 4, Tools" (The Right Instrument for the Job)
- Going after a task without tools is like going to **war without arms**.
- Ask yourself: "What will I need to accomplish my goal?"
  - The Bible for the minister.
  - Industry books and mentors for the entrepreneur.
  - Specific equipment for the specific craft.
  - Works of proven leaders in your chosen field.
- Acquire the Tools, no matter the cost.

## S12 — "Wisdom from the Spanner & the King" (Two Pictures That Make It Plain)
- **The Spanner** — try loosening a bolt with your bare hands. You will bleed and sweat, but the bolt will not move. There is a particular tool for the job.
- **The King** — "What king, going to make war against another king, sitteth not down first, and consulteth whether he be able?" (Luke 14:31)
- Dr. Oyedepo's own example: preparing a motivational drive across Africa, he first gathered the works of Norman Vincent Peale and Robert Schuller.
- Equip the **hand** before you attempt the **harvest**.

## S13 — "Kit 5, Timing" (Commanders Understand the Times)
- "To every thing there is a season, and a time to every purpose under the heaven." (Ecc 3:1)
- Jesus at Cana: "Mine hour is not yet come." (John 2:4)
- Jesus and Lazarus: He abode two more days. Timing produced resurrection, not mere healing.
- The seasonal business: selling iced water on a cold rainy day is a dead business on arrival.
- Right timing is a major asset. Until the time is ripe, do not step out.

## S14 — "How to Discern Timing" (Sensitivity Is the Skill)
- Timing has two edges: starting too early, and waiting too long. Both are failures of discernment.
- 4 Practical Disciplines for Reading your Season:
  1. **Ask God sincerely** — approach Him with a clear heart.
  2. **Remove distractions** — stand before Him expecting to hear.
  3. **Be sensitive to your environment** — resources are often right in your room.
  4. **Once green-lit, refuse contrary thoughts** — God provides when He commands.

## S15 — "Kit 6, Tillage" (Put Your Hands to Work)
- Hard work never kills; it only refines. Nothing kills like idleness.
- The future of any assignment is determined by the **Quality** of your labour input.
  - **The Foolish Farmer** — throws seed on unploughed ground, harvests nothing.
  - **The Oil Well** — no drill, no oil; everything precious requires pressure.
  - **The Teaching Tent** — start with two people; they will tell two more.
- "He that tilleth his land shall be satisfied with bread." (Pro 12:11)

## S16 — "Create Work, Don't Wait for It" (Disciplined Labour Beats Raw Effort)
- Tillage is not **random activity**; it is **disciplined**, **planned labour**:
  - Do not wait for perfect conditions.
  - Break your target into daily, doable actions.
  - **Prepare in Advance** (Winners prepares a full month's messages before the month begins).
  - Create work for yourself; do not wait for work to find you.
- "By much slothfulness the building decayeth; and through idleness of the hands the house droppeth through." (Ecc 10:18)

## S17 — "Kit 7, Tarrying" (Success Is Not a Seven-Day Wonder)
- God runs a **Due-season Calendar**. When you are due, He will not deny you.
- Between planted seed and gathered harvest, there is always a waiting period.
  - Rome was not built in a day.
  - A child born at six months is premature.
  - Every great manifestation requires a gestation period.
- "Though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come, it will not tarry." (Hab 2:3)

## S18 — "The Sledgehammer and Jericho" (The Cumulative Effect of Faithfulness)
- Your current blows are weakening the wall. Do Not Stop.
- **The Wall:** A man with a sledgehammer can't quit after one blow; the wall falls at the 50th, but it's the result of all 49 before it.
- **Jericho:** Israel marched for six days and nothing happened. On the seventh day, the walls fell. Same process. Different timing.
- When results tarry:
  - Do not give up.
  - Re-examine the kits; repair anything out of place.
  - Wait for the manifestation.

- **Training** — developing daily?
- **Target** — stated in one sentence?
- **Tools** — in your hand?
- **Timing** — the right season?
- **Tillage** — hands to the work?
- **Tarrying** — waiting with endurance?
