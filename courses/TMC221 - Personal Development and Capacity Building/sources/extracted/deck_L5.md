## S2 What Is Discouragement?

Subtitle: The slow leak that drains your courage to continue

Definition box (top of slide, before the list):
**Discouragement is the gradual withdrawal of the courage to continue.**

It shows up as (five items, two columns):
- **Academic pressure**: poor grades, imposter syndrome
- **Social comparison**: watching peers "have it all together"
- **Financial constraints** limiting your options
- **Career uncertainty**: "What do I do with this degree?"
- **Failure** in relationships, goals, or extracurricular pursuits

## S3 The Real Enemy

Two-column table:

|---|---|

## S5 The 50th Blow

Subtitle: Why the work you can't see is the most important work

Left panel, **The Sledgehammer Principle**: A man swings at a wall, blow after blow.
Nothing happens.
Blow 10 = Nothing.
Blow 30 = Nothing.
Blow 49 = Nothing.
**Blow 50 = the wall collapses.** (Dr Oyedepo)

Right list:
- It was not the **50th** blow that brought it down.
- It was the cumulative force of **ALL** the blows before it.
- The results you cannot see today are **being built by the work you are doing now.**
- Don't stop at blow 49.

## S10 Pillar 1: Vision in Practice

Subtitle: Run with precision, not just passion

Quote box: If you don't have a target, you'll keep following whatever is popular."
(Dr. Oyedepo)

**The Precision Test**, a goal must be:
- **Specific**: not "do better," but "achieve 4.90 GPA"
- **Measurable**: you can track it
- **Time-bound**: it has a deadline
- **Written**: if it is not written, it is a wish

Action panel: Write your top 3 goals for the next 12 months. That is your target. Now run.

## S11 Pillar 2: Information

Subtitle: Knowledge is the new currency of the world

Quote box: One is either building a library or building a mortuary without knowing.
(Dr. Oyedepo)

**The Reading Habit, The 1% Rule:**

| Action | Daily Time | Annual Output |
|---|---|---|
| Read non-academic books | 20 to 30 min | 12 to 18 books/year |
| Over 4 years of university | Daily | 48 to 72 books |

**Biblical Models of Lifelong Learners:**
- **Daniel**: remained relevant under 3 kings for 65 years
- **Paul the Apostle**: "Bring the books" (2 Tim. 4:13)

## S12 Pillar 3: Hard Work

Subtitle: There is no shortcut, perspiration is the price

Quote box: Until you're ready for the sweat, you're not set for the sweet."
(Dr. Oyedepo)

**The Alexander Kroll Story:**
- **Dreamed** of being a footballer: wrong physical build.
- **Solution:** Woke at **2:30 a.m. every night** to train.
- **Result:** Built the muscles. **Earned his place on the team.**

2. Every champion has **marks of failure** in their past record
3. **Opposition** is not a sign to stop, it is proof you are moving
4. The crown is on the other side of the endurance

Quote: "Winners never quit, and quitters never win." (Oral Robert)
