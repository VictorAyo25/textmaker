Kit 3: Target, certainty of goal

Teach: a target must be four thingsL4 S9
"If you don't have a goal, you will live like a goat, looking for the path of least resistance." (Dr. David Oyedepo.) A target must be: Specific (not vague), Defined (clearly described), Quantifiable (measurable progress), and Yours (born of your own calling). To live without a target is to end up in tears.
Teach: no goalpost, no gameL4 S10
However anointed or talented you are, without a goal you cannot score one. A football match without goalposts never ends. Without a target, you copy what is popular: your friend starts a crusade and you join, another opens a shop and you follow, a third returns to school and you enrol, and you end up disjointed. Always ask: "What am I actually after?" Stop fighting as one beating the air; run with the tape as your target.
Must MemoriseL4 S9, S10

Memory hook: no goalpost, no game. Specific, Defined, Quantifiable, Yours.

Kit 4: Tools, the right instrument for the job

Teach: war without armsL4 S11
Going after a task without tools is like going to war without arms. Ask, "What will I need to accomplish my goal?" The Bible for the minister; industry books and mentors for the entrepreneur; specific equipment for the specific craft; the works of proven leaders in your chosen field. Acquire the tools, no matter the cost.
Teach: the Spanner and the KingL4 S12
Two pictures. The Spanner: try loosening a bolt with your bare hands and you will bleed and sweat, but the bolt will not move, because there is a particular tool for the job. The King: "What king, going to make war against another king, sitteth not down first, and consulteth whether he be able?" (Luke 14:31.) Dr. Oyedepo's own example: preparing a motivational drive across Africa, he first gathered the works of Norman Vincent Peale and Robert Schuller. Equip the hand before you attempt the harvest.

Kit 5: Timing, commanders understand the times

Teach: a time for every purposeL4 S13
"To every thing there is a season, and a time to every purpose under the heaven." (Ecclesiastes 3:1.) Jesus at Cana: "Mine hour is not yet come." (John 2:4.) Jesus and Lazarus: he abode two more days, and timing produced resurrection, not mere healing. The seasonal business: selling iced water on a cold rainy day is a dead business on arrival. Right timing is a major asset; until the time is ripe, do not step out.
Teach: how to discern timingL4 S14
Timing has two edges: starting too early and waiting too long, and both are failures of discernment. Four practical disciplines for reading your season:
  1. Ask God sincerely: approach Him with a clear heart.
  2. Remove distractions: stand before Him expecting to hear.
  3. Be sensitive to your environment: resources are often right in your room.
  4. Once green-lit, refuse contrary thoughts: God provides when He commands.

Kit 6: Tillage, put your hands to work

Teach: hard work refinesL4 S15
Hard work never kills, it only refines; nothing kills like idleness. The future of any assignment is determined by the quality of your labour input. Three pictures: The Foolish Farmer throws seed on unploughed ground and harvests nothing; The Oil Well, no drill, no oil, everything precious requires pressure; The Teaching Tent, start with two people and they will tell two more. "He that tilleth his land shall be satisfied with bread." (Proverbs 12:11.)
Teach: create work, don't wait for itL4 S16
Tillage is not random activity, it is disciplined, planned labour: do not wait for perfect conditions; break your target into daily, doable actions; prepare in advance (a winner prepares a full month's messages before the month begins); and create work for yourself, do not wait for work to find you. "By much slothfulness the building decayeth; and through idleness of the hands the house droppeth through." (Ecclesiastes 10:18.)

Kit 7: Tarrying, success is not a seven-day wonder

Teach: the due-season calendarL4 S17
God runs a Due-season Calendar: when you are due, He will not deny you. Between planted seed and gathered harvest there is always a waiting period. Rome was not built in a day; a child born at six months is premature; every great manifestation requires a gestation period. "Though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come, it will not tarry." (Habakkuk 2:3.)
Teach: the Sledgehammer and JerichoL4 S18
Your current blows are weakening the wall, so do not stop. The Wall: a man with a sledgehammer cannot quit after one blow; the wall falls at the 50th, but it is the result of all 49 before it. Jericho: Israel marched for six days and nothing happened; on the seventh day the walls fell, same process, different timing. When results tarry: do not give up; re-examine the Kits and repair anything out of place; wait for the manifestation.
