Lecture 1: Goal Setting

  • Four levels: Wish (no direction), Dream (indefinite), Intention (no measure), Goal (specific, measurable, time-bound). Shift from "I want" to "I will, by [date], measured by [evidence]."
  • Three goal types: Outcome (what you achieve, outside daily control), Process (what you do, the daily engine), Identity (who you become, behaviour follows without negotiation).
  • Four frameworks and the failure each fixes: SMART+ER (no structure/emotion), OKRs (no measurable evidence), 12-Week Year (annual planning kills urgency), Implementation Intentions (no trigger to act).
  • SMART+ER: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound, plus Exciting and Reviewed.
  • OKR: one qualitative Objective + 2 to 4 measurable Key Results. A Key Result is not an activity. Adaeze scored 0.75 on posts (9 of 12), 0.5 on mentors (1 of 2), overall 0.76 (Strong).
  • 12-Week Year (Brian Moran): Vision, 12-Week Goal, Weekly Plan, Weekly Scorecard (target 85%+), Weekly Review. Short cycles keep urgency active.
  • Implementation Intentions (Gollwitzer): "When [concrete cue], I will [behaviour]." Cue = time/place/completed action, never emotional. 71% follow-through vs 32%.
  • Three reasons goals fail: Planning Fallacy (add 40% buffer), Motivation Decay (weeks 3 to 8), Willpower Depletion (design environment). None is laziness.
  • Six strategies: Habit Stacking, Temptation Bundling (+51%), 2-Minute Rule, Progress Principle (small wins), Accountability (95%), WOOP (Wish, Outcome, Obstacle, Plan; mental contrasting).
  • Total Man dimensions: Spiritual, Mental, Physical, Social, Financial.
  • 4-Layer Goal Stack: Life Vision (10 to 20 yrs), Strategic Goals (1 to 3 yrs), Quarterly Targets (12 wks), Weekly Actions (7 days).

Lecture 2: Positive Thinking and the W.I.S.E. Model

  • Five authors and books: Oyedepo (Winning Wisdom), Leaf (Switch On Your Brain), Peale (The Power of Positive Thinking), de Bono (Lateral Thinking), Young (A Technique for Producing Ideas).
  • W.I.S.E.: Wisdom (Oyedepo, seek the source), Inner Architecture (Leaf, prepare the receiver), Spirit of Possibility (Peale, fuel the engine), Execution (de Bono + Young). A cycle, not a one-time sequence.
  • Knowledge-Wisdom Ladder: Information, Knowledge, Wisdom (applying knowledge to results). Knowledge = power; wisdom = strength.
  • SWITCH Protocol (21-day, builds new neural structures): Gather, Focused Reflection, Write, Revisit, Active Reach, Celebrate. Not affirmations.
  • Fear: suppresses the prefrontal cortex (creative centre); fear and faith cannot coexist; fear is replaced, not managed.
  • de Bono's four tools: Random Entry, Provocative Operation (Po), Challenge Assumptions, Six Thinking Hats (White facts, Red emotion, Black caution, Yellow optimism, Green creativity, Blue process).
  • Young: an idea is a new combination of existing elements. Five steps: Gather, Digest, Drop, Eureka, Develop.

Lecture 3: Personal Branding

  • Your brand is what people say when you are not in the room (Bezos). Silence is invisibility, not neutrality.
  • Clean the inside first. Three dimensions of inner brand: Clarify Values, Character Consistency, Competence Depth.
  • Self-Audit: do well = zone of strength; care about = brand energy; others say = external reality; want to go = strategic direction. Ask 3 people for "three words."
  • Packaging (translation, not deception), three levels: Personal Presentation, Professional Documentation, Digital Presence.
  • Positioning: compete for Mindshare, not jobs (Ries and Trout). Chain: Depth of Focus, Distinction, Recall, Opportunity. Known for five = memorable for none.
  • Niche is a launch pad, not a limitation. Specialist = sought after; generalist = available.
  • Brand Bank Account: deposits (excellence, integrity, fulfilled promises, showing up); withdrawals (unreliability, poor output, public negativity, treating "unimportant" people poorly). Greatest threat = invisibility.
  • Authenticity = integration, producing trustworthiness. Networking = cultivating mutually beneficial relationships.

Lecture 4: Systems, the 7 Kits

  • System = parts working together to deliver a guaranteed result. Hard work without a system = engine with no transmission.
  • The 7 Kits (all T): Task (bound?), Training (equipped?), Target (aimed?), Tools (armed?), Timing (season?), Tillage (working?), Tarrying (waiting?).
  • Task: see it as necessity. Necessity to Invention to Distinction. Attempt ("let me try", retreat, dies at resistance) vs Task ("this must work", no retreat, outlasts opposition).
  • Training: Formal sets the pace; Informal (personal development) delivers the goods. Training determines placement.
  • Target must be Specific, Defined, Quantifiable, Yours. No goalpost, no game; run with the tape.
  • Tools: without them it is war without arms; acquire them no matter the cost. Spanner (right tool for the job), King (consult and prepare, Luke 14:31). Equip the hand before the harvest.
  • Timing: two edges, too early and too long. Right timing is a major asset; until ripe, do not step out.
  • Tillage: quality of labour decides the future; disciplined, planned labour; prepare in advance; create work, do not wait for it.
  • Tarrying: Due-season Calendar; gestation period; the wall falls at the 50th blow; Jericho fell on the seventh day. Do not stop.
