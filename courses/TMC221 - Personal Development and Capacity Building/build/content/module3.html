Teach: the three dimensions of inner brandL3 S5
"A good tree cannot bear bad fruit." (Matthew 7:18.) What cleaning the inside actually looks like:
  1. Clarify your Values: what would you refuse to compromise even if it cost you?
  2. Have Character Consistency: are you the same person with the professor and with the security guard?
  3. Build Competence Depth: every skill sharpened is a deposit the brand will one day withdraw.

Brand output is fruit, and fruit does not lie about the tree.

Module Three · Unit 2

Packaging, Positioning and Presence

Great character poorly communicated is invisible. This unit translates substance into signal, then positions it so, when a specific opening arises, your name comes to mind.

Teach: packaging, three levelsL3 S8
Great character poorly communicated equals invisible. Packaging has three levels:
  1. Personal Presentation: how you dress, carry yourself, and occupy a room. Your presence speaks before your mouth opens.
  2. Professional Documentation: your CV is not a list, it is a curated narrative. Every line must earn its place.
  3. Digital Presence: recruiters search you before they meet you; what they find is already forming an impression.

Packaging is not deception, it is translation.

Teach: authenticity is integrationL3 S14
One person, every room. The student who is respectful to professors but dismissive to staff, ambitious in interviews but passive in class, values-driven online but compromising in private, is not building a brand, they are performing one. Authenticity is integration: when private and public align, and words, actions, and values are the same person, you produce what no strategy can manufacture, trustworthiness. "Whatever you do, whether in word or deed, do it all in the name of the Lord Jesus." (Colossians 3:17.)
