Q1 · Single MCQLecture 1
A software developer feels energized about becoming a senior architect but has not clearly articulated what that role looks like month-to-month. She knows the direction but struggles with what measurable evidence would prove progress. Which approach best addresses her situation?
  • Attend more leadership seminars and networking events to learn what senior architects do
  • Set a one-year deadline and let timeline pressure create the clarity she needs
  • Separate her ambitious vision from measurable Key Results that prove progress, then tie Key Results to specific outcomes (not just activities)
  • Write the goal daily and visualise it, as repetition will eventually clarify the steps
Answer
c. The problem is missing measurable evidence, which is exactly what OKRs supply: an Objective plus Key Results that prove it, and Key Results are outcomes, not activities.
