How to use this manual

This manual teaches PHY121 from the ground up. It is written so that you can work through it without the slides open beside you. Everything the slides present is here, in the same order, but with the reasoning filled in: the steps that were spoken aloud in class rather than typed onto the slide, and the background a newcomer needs before the first equation makes sense. Physics is not a memory subject with a few formulas bolted on. It is a small number of ideas, each used over and over. The whole of this course rests on roughly a dozen of them. Once you can state those dozen in your own words, the several hundred slides collapse into something you can hold in your head.

The box system

Every box carries a labelled header and always means the same thing, so you can navigate by shape and colour instead of reading everything twice.

How to actually study this

1. Do not read passively. Reading physics feels productive and teaches almost nothing. The RECALL boxes exist to force retrieval, which is what actually builds memory. Cover the answer before you look at it, every time. 2. Redo every worked example on blank paper. Not "follow along and nod" but close the manual and reproduce it. Where you get stuck is exactly the thing you did not understand, and you will not discover it any other way.
