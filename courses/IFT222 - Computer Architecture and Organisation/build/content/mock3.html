Question Paper

Time: 2 hours. Answer Question One (compulsory, 30 marks) and any other two questions (20 marks each). Total 70 marks.

These questions were written for this manual. They are not a real paper. Every number in them is new and deliberately harder than anything that has been set, so none of them carries an As printed bar: that bar is reserved for the examiner's own words, and nothing on this page is the examiner's. What this paper copies is the shape of the real papers, and it goes one step past them on purpose, so that the genuine article feels easier than your last rehearsal. When you want the real wording, go to the Past Papers part, where 2024/25 and 2025/26 are quoted in full and answered.

Exam questionQuestion Two (20 marks)

(a) A 5-stage pipeline has stage times 40, 55, 45, 60 and 50 ns with a 5 ns latch delay. Find (i) the cycle time, (ii) the non-pipelined time for one task, (iii) the ideal speedup, (iv) the pipelined and sequential times for 800 tasks, and (v) the steady-state throughput. (10 marks)

(b) Name and explain the three classes of pipeline hazard, and state which one forwarding cannot remove. (4 marks)

(c) A compiler optimisation speeds up 75% of a program by a factor of 8. Find the overall speedup by Amdahl's law, and explain why it is not 8. (6 marks)

Exam questionQuestion Three (20 marks)

(a) A cache of 32 KB with 64-byte blocks backs a 16 MB main memory, 8-way set associative. Find the number of lines, the number of sets, the address format, and the size of the tag directory. (10 marks)

(b) The cache has a hit ratio of 92%, an access time of 3 ns, and main memory takes 70 ns. Find the average memory access time under hierarchical and under simultaneous organisation. (6 marks)

(c) Explain locality of reference, and distinguish temporal from spatial locality with one example of each. (4 marks)

Exam questionQuestion Four (20 marks)

(a) Use end-around carry in one's complement to compute 245 − 178 in a 10-bit register. Show every step. (6 marks)

(b) (i) Encode 507 in Excess-3. (ii) Decode 0111 1001 0110 from Excess-3. (iii) State the property that makes Excess-3 self-complementing. (4 marks)

(c) Write Y = (P + Q) × (R − S) / T in the 3, 2, 1 and 0 address formats, and count the bytes of each, taking 1 byte per opcode and 2 bytes per address. (10 marks)

Exam questionQuestion Five (20 marks)

(a) Find the decimal value of C2C60000H under IEEE-754 single precision. (6 marks)

(b) A computer uses 21 address lines and a word size of 4 bytes, word-addressable. Find the address space, the total memory in MB, and the width of the program counter. (5 marks)

(c) Find the memory needed for a black-and-white image 8.5 by 11 inches at 200 dpi, in KB. (4 marks)

(d) State four differences between RISC and CISC, and name two example processors of each. (5 marks)
