--- NOTES ON THE PAPER'S OWN DEFECTS (observations, not corrections) ---
1. Question Three carries NO mark total in its heading. Every other question on
   the paper states one. Its parts do sum to 20.
2. Question Two and Question Five are headed "(20marks)" with no space;
   Question Four is headed "(20 marks)" with one.
3. Q1a numbers its sub-items "i.", "ii.", then "iii)" - the third switches from
   a full stop to a closing bracket.
4. Q1b(ii) spaces its equals signs inconsistently: "stage1= 8ns", "stage2 = 9ns",
   "stage5 =16ns".
5. Q3a's Table 1 gives the PC Relative row a remark beginning "R5 = 0x01700"
   although that row's mode is PC relative, not register indirect; the register
   named looks wrong for the mode.
6. Q4c is labelled "c." and then opens with "ii)" although there is no "i)".
7. Q5a(ii) reads "in an 10-bit register". The identical question on the 23/24
   and 24/25 papers reads "in a 10-bit register".
8. Q5b(i) and Q5b(ii) are commands punctuated as questions ("List four (4)
   differences between DRAM and SRAM?", "Show details of work?").
9. Q3b's third row, "1111 0110 11", is 10 bits and the instruction says "Use
   10bits", so this paper's version of that table is self-consistent. The 23/24
   and 24/25 papers reuse the same table shape with an 11-bit row under a
   12-bit instruction.
