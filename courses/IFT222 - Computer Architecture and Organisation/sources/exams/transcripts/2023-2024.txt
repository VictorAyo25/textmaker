--- NOTES ON THE PAPER'S OWN DEFECTS (observations, not corrections) ---
1. Q1b reads "State Amdahl's law'" with a stray closing apostrophe. The 25/26
   paper reproduces the same stray apostrophe, so it is copied forward between
   years rather than a scanning artefact.
2. Q1c asks which of the claims "is true or false". The 24/25 paper sets the
   identical question but asks only which "is true". The instruction verb
   differs between the two years even though the data are identical.
3. Q1d: "8-way set association cache" (association, not associative), and the
   sentence is a fragment with no question in it; the four sub-items supply it.
4. Q3a ends "...main memory access time of 100 ns when." with a full stop where
   the list needs a colon.
5. Q3c(iii) reads "Spacial Locality". The 24/25 paper writes the same term as
   "Special Locality". Neither is "spatial".
6. Q4b says "Use 12bits." (no space) and the third table row "1101 00110 11" is
   11 bits. The 24/25 paper repeats the same row with the same shortfall, so the
   defect is copied forward.
7. Q5b is worth "4mk", singular.
8. Q5d and 24/25 Q4b(i) are the same question; both use an en dash in
   "153 – 142". The 20/21 paper's version reads "in an 10-bit register".
