--- NOTES ON THE PAPER'S OWN DEFECTS (observations, not corrections) ---
1. Q1c numbers its four sub-items a./b./c./d., which collide with the
   question's own top-level parts a./b./c./d. Both labelled "c." and "d." exist
   on the same page meaning different things.
2. Q2b lists "Special Locality". The intended term is spatial locality. The
   23/24 paper misspells the same word differently ("Spacial Locality"), so the
   misspelling is a habit of the setter, not a one-off.
3. Q3b says "Use 12 bits" but the third table row, "1101 00110 11", contains
   only 11 bits. The student must decide what to do about this in the hall.
4. Q4a(i) is worth "3mk", singular, where every other part uses "mks".
5. Q4b(i) is phrased as a command but ends in a question mark.
6. Q5c reads "Perform the calculation below in using Single Precision IEEE
   format" - "in using" is the paper's wording.
7. Q4 is headed "(20 Marks)" with a capital M; Questions One, Two, Three and
   Five use "(20 marks)"/"(30 marks)" lower case.
8. Marks are annotated inconsistently: "1mk" vs "1mks", and a space appears in
   "1½ mks" (Q2b ii) but not in "4½mks" (Q2b i).
