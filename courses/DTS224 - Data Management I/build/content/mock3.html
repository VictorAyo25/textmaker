Question Paper

DTS224 Data Management I. Time: 2 hours. Attempt any four (4) questions. Each carries 25 marks.

Question One

(a) Distinguish disjoint from overlap, and total from partial specialization. (6)

(b) A PUBLICATION (pubID, title) is either a BOOK (isbn) or a JOURNAL (issn), never both, and every publication is one of the two. A BOOK is further either a TEXTBOOK (subject) or a NOVEL (genre). Draw the EER diagram with all constraints. (19)

Question Two

(a) State the BCNF condition. (3)

(b) Teaching(student, course, teacher) has (student, course) → teacher and teacher → course. Find all candidate keys, state the highest normal form it satisfies, and decompose it to BCNF. (22)

Question Three

Over Student(sID, sName, level), Course(cID, cTitle, unit), Enrol(sID, cID, score): write SQL to (i) list students with their course titles; (ii) count students per course; (iii) students who scored above the average score; (iv) courses no student is enrolled in; (v) the highest score in each course. (25)

Question Four

(a) Define, with an example, the division operation of relational algebra. (5)

(b) Over Takes(sID, cID) and Offered(cID, dept): write relational algebra for the students who have taken every course offered by the 'CIS' department. (12)

(c) Write the algebra for the names of all students, given Student(sID, sName). (8)

Question Five

A delivery company handles PARCELs for CUSTOMERs. A customer sends many parcels; each parcel is sent by exactly one customer. Each parcel is carried by one COURIER, and a courier carries many parcels. A courier is assigned to one ROUTE; a route has many couriers. Draw the ER diagram with identifiers and cardinalities. (25)

Question Six

(a) Explain the relationship between a candidate key and a foreign key, and state the referential integrity rule. (9)

(b) Transform the ER model of Question Five into a set of relations, showing all primary and foreign keys. (16)
