Module Five · Unit 1

Relational Algebra

A small set of operations that each take one or two relations and produce a new relation. Chain them and you can express any query. The exam asks you to define the operations and to write expressions, so this unit does both.

By the end of this unit you can: define the six core operations with their symbols, and write a relational-algebra expression for any query over a given schema.

1.1 The operations and their symbols

Must memoriseSix operations, plus two
OperationSymbolDoes
Selectionσ (sigma)picks rows that meet a condition: σprice<40(Room)
Projectionπ (pi)picks columns: πname,city(Hotel)
Union∪all rows in either relation (same columns), duplicates removed
Set difference−rows in the first relation but not the second
Cartesian product×every row of one paired with every row of the other
Join⋈ (bowtie)a product kept only where a matching condition holds

Two more you will use: rename ρ (rho), which gives a relation or its columns a new name, and division ÷, which finds rows associated with all rows of another relation ("works on all projects").

WorkedPQ 25/26 Q4b · PQ 15/16 Q5a

Q. Explain six relational algebra operations with examples (15/16 asks for the three basic ones).

The three basic ones the older paper wants are selection (σ, choose rows), projection (π, choose columns), and join (⋈, combine two relations on a match). The full six add union (∪, combine two union-compatible relations), set difference (−, rows in one but not the other), and Cartesian product (×, all pairings). Each takes relations and returns a relation, which is why they chain.

1.2 Worked: College, Student, Apply

Exam questionPQ 24/25 Q1b

College(C_Name, State, Enrollment); Student(S_ID, S_Name, CGPA, HighSch_Size); Apply(S_ID, College_Name, Course, Decision). Write in relational algebra: (i) students with CGPA > 4.5; (ii) with CGPA > 4.5 and high-school size < 50; (iii) names of students who applied to CST for Computer Science; (iv) names and CGPA of students with high-school size > 1000 who applied for Computer Science at a college with enrollment > 20000 and were rejected.

Answer
(i)   σ CGPA > 4.5 (Student)

(ii)  σ CGPA > 4.5 ∧ HighSch_Size < 50 (Student)

(iii) π S_Name ( Student ⋈ σ College_Name='CST' ∧ Course='Computer Science' (Apply) )

(iv)  π S_Name, CGPA (
        σ HighSch_Size > 1000 (Student)
        ⋈ σ Course='Computer Science' ∧ Decision='Reject' (Apply)
        ⋈ σ Enrollment > 20000 (ρ College_Name ← C_Name (College)) )

The joins in (iii) and (iv) match on S_ID (Student to Apply) and on the college name; the rename in (iv) lines up College's C_Name with Apply's College_Name so the join has a common attribute. ∧ is "and".

1.3 Worked: Cartesian product and rename

Exam questionPQ 24/25 Q1c

Boys(Name, State) = {(Adamu, Kogi), (Obi, Imo)}. Girls(Name, State) = {(Kemi, Kwara), (Hadiza, Kaduna), (Chioma, Rivers)}. (i) Perform the Cartesian product; (ii) rename the result as people.

Answer

(i) Boys × Girls pairs every boy row with every girl row, so 2 × 3 = 6 rows, each with four columns (renamed to avoid the clash on Name and State):

B.Name  B.State  G.Name   G.State
Adamu   Kogi     Kemi     Kwara
Adamu   Kogi     Hadiza   Kaduna
Adamu   Kogi     Chioma   Rivers
Obi     Imo      Kemi     Kwara
Obi     Imo      Hadiza   Kaduna
Obi     Imo      Chioma   Rivers

(ii) Rename the whole result:   people ← ρ (Boys × Girls).

1.4 Worked: employees, departments, projects (with division)

Exam questionPQ 25/26 Q4c

Emp(Fname, Lname, SSN, Dno); Dept(DName, DNum, Mgr_SSN); D_Loc(DNo, Location); Project(PName, PNo, DNo, P_Loc); Works_on(ESSN, PNo, Hours). Write: (i) names of all employees who work for the Headquarters department; (ii) for every project located in Abuja, the project number, controlling department, and manager's name; (iii) names of employees who work on all projects controlled by DNo = A3/4.

Answer
(i)   π Fname, Lname (
        Emp ⋈ Dno=DNum σ DName='Headquarters' (Dept) )

(ii)  π PNo, DName, Fname, Lname (
        σ P_Loc='Abuja' (Project)
        ⋈ DNo=DNum Dept
        ⋈ Mgr_SSN=SSN Emp )

(iii) Works on ALL projects of A3/4 is a DIVISION:
        A34   ← π PNo ( σ DNo='A3/4' (Project) )
        EP    ← π ESSN, PNo ( Works_on )
        Result ← π Fname, Lname ( (EP ÷ A34) ⋈ ESSN=SSN Emp )

Division is the "for all" operator: EP ÷ A34 returns the ESSNs that appear with every PNo in A34, that is, the employees working on all of that department's projects.
