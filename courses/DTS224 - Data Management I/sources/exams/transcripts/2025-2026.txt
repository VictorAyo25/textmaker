[[Q5b]]
b. Develop an EER model for the following situation, using the crowfoot notation.   (11 mks)

   A technology company provides offerings to its customers. Offerings are of two separate types:
   products and services. Offerings are identified by an offering ID and an attribute of description. In
   addition, products are described by product name, standard price, and date of first release; services
   are described by name of the company's unit responsible for the service and conditions of service.
   There are repair, maintenance, and other types of services. A repair service has a cost and is the repair
   of some product; a maintenance service has an hourly rate. Fortunately, some products never require
   repair. However, there are many potential repair services for a product.

   A customer may purchase an offering, and the company needs to keep track of when the offering was
   purchased and the contact person for that offering with the customer. Unfortunately, not all offerings
   are purchased. Customers are identified by customer ID and have descriptive data of name, address,
   and phone number. When a service is performed, that service is billed to some customer. Because
   some customers purchase offerings for their clients, a customer may be billed for services he or she
   did not purchase, as well as for ones that were purchased. When a customer is billed for a service
   (although some may never require a service of any type), the company needs to keep track of the date
   the service was performed, the date the bill is due, and the amount due.

   the examiner's, not a transcription slip:
     - most questions use "mks" and a vulgar fraction: (17 ½ mks), (2½ mks), (1½ mks)
     - Question Three alone switches to the word "marks" AND a decimal:
       (5 marks), (2.5 marks), (10 marks)

   Every one of these is reproduced exactly.

5. Q6(b) Table 1 heads its last column "staffName", but every functional dependency
   below it calls that attribute "sName". The relation as printed therefore names
   the same attribute two ways. This is a real contradiction in the question and is
   left standing; a student must decide which to use and say so.

6. Q6(b) fd3 (staffNo → sName) is labelled "(Transitive dependency)" by the paper.
   Against the stated primary key (propertyNo, iDate), staffNo is a non-key
   attribute, so this is indeed the transitive step; but fd4 (staffNo, iDate →
   carReg) is labelled "(Transitive dependency)" too while being determined by a
   composite that includes part of the key. The labels are the paper's and are
   quoted as printed; the breakdown discusses them rather than correcting them.

7. Q1(c) and Q6 use the Naira sign ₦ (U+20A6) before the amounts: ₦100,000 and
   ₦80,000. This is a meaningful glyph and is transcribed as the character, not
