NOTES ON THE PAPER'S OWN DEFECTS AND ODDITIES (mine, not the paper's)

1. Q3(a) Table 1 dates: two rows read "14-Sep-98", where every other row reads
   "-08". A 1998 appointment sitting between 2008 ones is almost certainly a
   typing slip in the paper, but it is printed and is left standing; it does not
   change the normalisation.

2. Q3(a) fifth dependency begins "Date, ATime, SurgeonNo" where every other line
   says "ADate". There is no attribute called Date. Also, the candidate keys listed
   above the table spell it "Atime" while the dependencies spell it "ATime".

3. Q3(a) prints "StaffNo &#8594; DentistName  Td" with the tag unbracketed where the
   others are "(pk)", "(pd)", "(ck)", and the fourth dependency carries no tag.

4. Q6(d) states its own marks TWICE and contradicts itself: "(6marks)" in the stem
   and "[4 Marks]" after part (iv). The paper totals only if it is 4, since
   3 + 6 + 4.5 + 4 = 17.5.

5. Q6(d)(iii) prints "any two ( or even three f these subtypes": an unclosed
   bracket and "f" for "of". Reproduced exactly.

6. Q6(d)(iv) ends "one of these subtype", singular.

7. Q1(a) prints "(5) five" and Q1(b) "(3) three" style duplication of the number.

8. The mark text alternates between a space and none: "[6Marks]", "[6.5Marks]" but
   "[5 Marks]", "[3 Marks]".

9. Q5(a) (semantic objects), Q6(a) and Q6(b) (information efficiency/effectiveness,
   information life cycle) are outside the DTS224 syllabus; they belong to the older
   "High Performance AND Database Management System" title this paper carries.

10. The same ConsultCo business-rule narrative appears as 2(b) here and as 2(b) in
    the 2015/2016 paper. The wording differs by one phrase: this paper asks for
    "entities, relationships, optionalities and cardinalities", where 2015/2016 asks
    for "entities, relationships, optionalities, connectivities and cardinalities".
